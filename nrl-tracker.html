Prediction Model

ELO + Form Hybrid Model

Predictions combine multiple weighted factors derived from live NRL data:

  • 30% Ladder position & competition points
  • 25% Points differential (attack vs defence)
  • 20% Recent form (last 5 games W/L streak)
  • 15% Home/away advantage & ground record
  • 10% Head-to-head implied by win margins

Win probability uses a logistic function applied to the composite rating difference. Predicted margins scale linearly with probability gap.

Data sourced live from nrl.com via proxy. Refreshes every 60 seconds.
